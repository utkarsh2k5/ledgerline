Investment commentary

Over the quarter the Scheme's assets returned 4.06%, behind the benchmark return of 4.32% by 0.25%. Over one year the fund returned 11.76% against a benchmark of 12.44%, underperforming by 0.68%. Relative to benchmark, UK Property was the strongest mandate this quarter, ahead by 0.43%, while Diversified Growth lagged by 1.45%. Attribution over one year shows asset allocation added 0.21% and manager selection detracted 0.79%. The funding ratio stands at 93.40%, a deficit of £108.6m, and the interest-rate hedge covers 81.96% of the liabilities' sensitivity to rates. In our stress tests, a fall of one percentage point in interest rates would reduce the funding ratio by 3.18 percentage points, while a fall of a fifth in growth asset values would reduce it by 10.91 percentage points.
